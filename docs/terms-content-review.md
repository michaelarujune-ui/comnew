# Website terms content review

This is a disclosure check of the current repository. It is not a determination that the site is outside financial-promotion rules, and it is not approval to publish.

## Confirmed operator

NOVARIN CAPITAL is the visitor-facing brand in `src/config/site.ts`. No registered entity, company number, address, or statement of who operates the site is on file. The brand is not evidence of a legal controller or of authority to bind a fund or affiliate.

## Intended activities and audience

The pages introduce an investment focus on early-stage companies connecting stablecoins with payment and settlement networks, and they invite a general inquiry. There is no account opening, subscription flow, custody, or payment execution.

## Solicitation and transactions

No investment-subscription, account, or transactional feature is implemented. Homepage and Contact forms are disabled previews. The newsletter is disabled and omitted from a publishable build. An inquiry is not a funding commitment; Contact and Approach copy already say outcomes are not guaranteed.

## Ownership and licensing

- Visitor copy is authored in this repository. Ownership of that copy is not documented.
- No photographs are published. Image slots stay empty until a licensed file is supplied.
- The wordmark is set in type, not a separate logo file.
- Cormorant Garamond and Source Sans 3 are bundled from `@fontsource`. Each package’s LICENSE file states the SIL Open Font License 1.1. That is a font license, not a claim that Novarin owns the fonts.
- No third-party article or trademark license is recorded.

## Inquiries, confidentiality, and contact

Forms do not send or store entries. No confidentiality agreement, data room, or response-time promise is implemented. No public email or postal address is configured. A disabled form is not a working legal-contact channel.

## Existing notices and agreements

The Privacy Notice at `/privacy` is a review preview. `privacyPublicationApproved` is false. No earlier terms or separate investment agreement is in the repository. Terms revision `review-01` is unapproved: `termsPublicationApproved` is false, `termsApprovedRevision` and `termsEffectiveDate` are null, and `approvedTermsSections` is empty.

## Claims checked on the marketing pages

No contradiction was found between a claimed completed investment and the stated focus. The pages describe a focus and a process. They do not name portfolio companies, completed deals, staff appointments, returns, liquidity, funding amounts, or regulatory permissions.

Leadership profiles and Perspectives articles are drafts. Public mode with nothing approved shows empty states rather than those drafts.

Contact says an inquiry does not guarantee an investment review or funding. Approach says support outcomes and further investment are not guaranteed. Neither page promises a response time.

No page states a regulator, license, or exemption. Geographic language is about the investment focus (“across borders and in local markets”), not a claim of regulatory passporting.

These findings are from the repository copy only. They do not audit an external firm, hosting account, or unpublished materials.
